CodePen 2.0 Is Here

The Classic Editor evolved for a dozen years. Version 2.0 is a complete rewrite — still familiar, but ready for real project work in the browser.

June 12, 2026 · By Chris Coyier

CodePen 2.0 balances the simplicity people loved about classic Pens with a more powerful coding experience: a real file system, a new build pipeline called Blocks, deployment, and collaboration baked into the editor itself. You can get the overview at codepen.io/2 .

Files & folders, finally

Pens are no longer stuck with a single HTML, CSS, and JS pane. You can add, rename, and organize files and folders — open as many tabs as you need across up to three editors. Legacy Projects became 2.0 Pens; every Pen now has that file-system power built in.

Blocks instead of one-off settings

Blocks are the technologies in the build process — Sass, TypeScript, Markdown, Prettier, PostCSS, and friends. Mix and match what you need; the CodePen Compiler wires them together. Often you just use the right extension ( .scss , .tsx , .md ) and the Compiler adds the Block for you. No more picking a single preprocessor and calling it a day.

Find anything with the Omnibar

The search box at the top is the fast pass to files, Blocks, settings, and shortcuts. If something moved from the Classic Editor, search for it there. Prefer a quieter chrome? Turn on Minimal UI to tuck the sidebar and footer away until you need them.

Privacy & collaboration, rebuilt

Pens can be private (unguessable URL, hidden from browse and search), password-protected, or limited to people you invite. Collaboration no longer needs a special Collab URL — invite editors from the Privacy & Collaboration panel and work in realtime, or leave changes for someone who comes online later.

It’s a big jump from the Classic Editor, but the goal is the same: make front-end prototyping and sharing feel immediate — just with room to grow when a Pen becomes something more.
